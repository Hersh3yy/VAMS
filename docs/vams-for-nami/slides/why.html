Why it exists

One place, not ten copies

The old way

Every website keeps its own words and pictures glued inside it. Change one photo? You open each site and fix it again and again.

The VAMS way

All the words and pictures live in one box. Each site just asks the box. Fix the photo once, and every site shows the new one.

Programmers call a box like this a headless CMS — a content store with no shop window of its own, so many shops can borrow from it.

A content-management system (CMS) normally has its own website. VAMS is "headless": it has no front of its own, it just feeds other sites. Ask Nami: what goes wrong if the same photo is copied into ten places and you forget one?
