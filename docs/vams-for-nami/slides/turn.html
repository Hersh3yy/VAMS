Invent a card shape

Pick something you love — books, sneakers, your playlists. Now design its card: write the blanks every card must fill in.

A "book card" might be: title · author · stars out of 5 · finished? That is exactly what Hiren does to teach VAMS a new kind of thing.

End on a game. Let Nami design one Entry Type out loud, then fill two real cards of that shape. Ask: which blank is the one you would never want to leave empty?
