Filled cards

The real filled-in cards. Their answers sit in one roomy pocket.

table: entries (content)

The roomy pocket (programmers call it a JSON column) is why one card can hold a whole colour palette without spilling. Where this stops being like a spreadsheet: a pocket can hold a list inside a list, which a flat cell cannot.

This is the shape of the data (an ERD) drawn as three linked boxes. Owners own cards; every card follows a shape. Ask Nami: in a spreadsheet, how would you link a "pets" tab to an "owners" tab so you know whose pet is whose?
