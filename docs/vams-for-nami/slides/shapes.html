The clever bit

You design the card, then fill many

First you decide what shape a card has: what blanks it must fill in. Then you make as many cards of that shape as you like. VAMS does not force one fixed shape on you — you invent it.

Step 1 · the shape

"An event card has…"

a title · a date · a place · a line-up · a ticket link

Programmers' name: an Entry Type.

Step 2 · the cards

Fill in hundreds

One filled card per real event, each with its own title, date and place.

Programmers' name: an Entry.

This is the heart of VAMS: you invent the shape (Entry Type), then make many filled cards (Entries). It is like designing a form, then collecting lots of answers. Ask Nami: if she made a "pet card" shape, what three blanks would it have?
